# Chapter 19: How to ACEL

## How to Accelerate

## On Purpose

Accelerating is to grasp our ultimate purpose: the betterment of humanity, genetically and memetically. We are collaborators in a billion-year process of evolution, stamping our influence on the next generation, and the next one after it. Here, only the best ideas survive—good ideas prosper, bad ideas die. This is the organic selection that forms our legacy.

The role thus stands clear: to contribute, to propagate, either through potent memes, or resilient genes, each carrying a long, time-enduring half-life. True fulfillment lies in this legacy—the relentless pursuit of influence that extends beyond the self, forging a lineage that stands resilient against time.

## On Wealth

This mindset, therefore, stands resistant to individual pursuits towards the accumulation of wealth: as it is driven by an instinct of fear and of lack, the goal of accumulation of wealth is at its outset bound for failure and stagnation, and thus presents as static, which is against the natural flow of the cosmos. That is to say, the struggle to amass wealth, status, or power is void of any lasting meaning if they are not directed towards nor contributive to the survival and evolution of our species, which is best affected by the growth of our memetic repositories.

True wealth lies in the ability to create and share powerful ideas; to spread potent memes and genes; to embrace fluidity and adaptions; to increase the intelligence of our species and to empower others to agency. The agents of change are those who choose to do this. Those are the ACELs—always moving, constantly pivoting, constantly influencing.

## On Process

The nature of acceleration is logarithmic—efforts snowball, and effects compound. History shows us that humanity needs but a spark—one individual to ignite a movement. From there, anything becomes possible. Anyone can be a catalyst. Every action holds the potential to amplify.

Acels understand that coherent output—genetic or memetic—is the ultimate market of success. One must stop seeking reasons to hold back. In this pursuit, hesitation is the only enemy. Begin accelerating, move with relentless determination and unshakable optimism; forward motion is the true path to transformation.

## On Building

Build in public—build to create, not to hoard. Those who build in secret are trapped in the consumer’s loop, amassing value only to consume more. They have yet to escape this cycle; they are merely spectators, decelerating humanity.

Building in public is essential. It is how we contribute with urgency and impact, expanding the collective memetic repository. Patents, trademarks, copyrights, closed-source information—these are the brakes on progress, barriers that stifle the evolution of ideas. True acceleration lies in open contribution, in pushing the frontier forward without restraint.
